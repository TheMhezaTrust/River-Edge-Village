# User Guide — The Mheza Trust Website & Workstation

## Part A: Public Website Users

### Finding a plot
1. Open the home page and click **View Available Plots**, or use **Projects → River Edge Rural Village**.
2. On the project page, the **interactive farm map** shows all 216 plots, with erf numbers matching the official survey layout plan: green = available, yellow = reserved, red = sold. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click any plot for details and pricing. An **Official Site Plan** toggle shows the surveyor's drawing itself.
3. The **Plots** page offers a searchable, filterable list (grid or table view).
4. Click **View Details** on any plot for its full page: size, road frontage, pricing and payment options.

### Expressing interest / buying
- Click **Express Interest** or **Buy This Plot** and complete the form (name, ID/passport, email, phone). Your preferred plot is pre-filled.
- After submitting you receive an on-screen confirmation; a consultant contacts you within 24–48 hours. Your inquiry is logged in the Trust's workstation for follow-up.
- To visit the farm, use **Contact Us → Schedule a Site Viewing** and pick a date and number of attendees.

### Checking project status
The public **Status** page shows the project timeline and public announcements filterable by category. Detailed government department engagement records (contact officials, direct numbers, internal notes and follow-up dates) are confidential and only visible to staff inside the Workstation.

### Member portal (your account is created for you, then you log in)
1. **Your account is created by the Trust office.** There is no public sign-up form. To get portal access, contact the Trust by phone (**081 391 7967**) or email (**themhezatrust@gmail.com**); staff verify your identity, set up your member record and issue your portal login credentials.
2. **Log in** at **Login → Member** (`/login`) or `/portal` using the email and password the Trust office gave you. Change your password after your first sign-in.
3. A consultant confirms your plot allocation and records your payments; your balance and documents appear once that is done.
4. **You must be logged in to see any of your financial or personal information.** Nothing about your payments, balance or documents is visible to the public or to other members. In the portal you can:
   - See your plot details and a **payment progress bar** (paid vs outstanding balance)
   - Review your full **payment history**
   - **Upload documents**: certified ID copy, beneficiary nomination form, building plans
   - Download documents you have uploaded (each file is only reachable through your own signed-in session)
   - Download your **Certificate of Land Use** when it becomes available
   - Update your contact details and change your password
   - Track project progress, read member announcements and review the **Terms and Conditions of Sale** that govern your Right of Use
5. Payments are made by EFT to the project's official Absa account — **River Edge SA Primary Co-operative Ltd**, acc **4121013447**, branch **632005** (shown in the portal and on **Trust Info**). Always use the mandatory reference format `Plot Number – Buyer Name` (for example `12 – Your Full Name`), as set out in clause 6.4 of the Terms and Conditions of Sale — payments without it cannot be allocated.
6. Sessions expire automatically after 12 hours of inactivity; simply log in again.

### Terms and Conditions of Sale
The full 21-clause **Terms and Conditions of Sale** are published at `/terms` (also linked from the footer, the nav bar, Trust Info and every plot page). They cover the Right of Use, pricing and the non-payment ladder, the Right of Use Certificate and transfer fee, beneficiary nomination, the Community Constitution, building setbacks and deadlines, infrastructure requirements, annual levies, privacy, dispute resolution and termination. Read them before paying a deposit.

### Verifying the Trust
The **Trust Info** page lists the Trust registration number (IT000099/2024(E)), trustees, a deed summary, and official banking details. All The Mheza Trust projects are administered by **River Edge Primary Co-Op**. The Trust will never ask you to pay into a personal account. Verify by phone before paying.

## Part B: Workstation Users (Staff)

### Signing in
- URL: `/login` and choose **Administrator / Staff** (or go directly to `/workstation`) — authorized personnel only. All actions are recorded in the audit log.
- Use your work email and password. After 12 hours your session expires.
- **Every role can open and read every module.** What differs is what you can *change*: action buttons are hidden automatically when your role cannot edit that area.

### Who can change what

| Role | People | Can change |
|---|---|---|
| Administrator | Bongani Sifiniza | Everything, including staff accounts, settings and the audit log |
| Finance & Member Records | Thandile Sifiniza, Sisanda Toni, Nokuthula Gedle | Finances, payroll, member/customer records, inquiries, the document library, member communications and announcements |
| Plot Administrator | Sipho Jauka, Sydney Velapi, Lihle Jacob | Plot records: erf number, size, availability, price, block and layout position |

All three roles can view every module (members, plots, finance, documents, departments, inquiries, tasks, communication, reports, settings) and can update their own tasks. Where you cannot edit, the page shows a **"Read-only view"** banner and the add/edit/delete buttons are not rendered.

### Dashboard
Shows quick stats (plots by status, members, outstanding balances, monthly revenue, sales progress), a 12-month sales trend chart, quick actions, your notifications, your assigned tasks, and recent team activity.

### Members
- **Members** — search/filter all members; export CSV; add a member with full personal, plot and payment-plan details; open a member for their complete record.
- On a member's page: record payments (auto-creates an income entry and notifies the team), edit details, view uploaded documents, beneficiaries and notes.

### Plots
- Map and table views of all 216 plots, numbered per the official survey layout plan (including subdivided erven such as 1A, 145A/145B, 165A, 186A and 203A). Click a plot to change status (Available / Reserved / Sold), edit size, price or details.
- Select multiple rows to bulk-update status or price.

### Inquiries
- All website and phone inquiries land here. Assign to a team member, change status (New → Contacted → Converted/Closed), set follow-up dates, and **convert to member** (pre-fills the member record and reserves the plot).

### Finance
- **Income / Expenses**: record and review all transactions with categories and totals.
- **Payments**: outstanding balances per member with full payment history.
- **Cash Flow**: 12-month inflow/outflow chart, closing balances, and P&L summary.
- **Payroll**: employees with PAYE/UIF/SDL deductions, net pay, and printable payslips.

### Documents
Central library with folders (Trust, Project, Legal, Financial, Member, Department, Minutes), search by title/tag, upload with optional role-based visibility, download links.

### Departments
Every government engagement (BCMM, DALRRD, Sanitation, Waterworks, IEM, City Health, DWA, DEDEA, Roads, Engineering) with contact person, status, follow-up dates and a communication log. Log every email, call or meeting here — it feeds the public Status page narrative.

### Tasks
List, drag-and-drop **Kanban**, and calendar views. Assign tasks, set priorities and due dates; overdue items are highlighted; assignees get notified.

### Communication
Internal messages between staff (inbox/sent/drafts), team announcements (Administrator and Finance & Member Records roles), and copy-ready templates for payment reminders and follow-ups.

### Reports
Sales by month, inquiry conversion funnel, member payment status, expense breakdown, and CSV export.

### Settings
Open to every role: the staff directory (read-only unless you are an Administrator), the role permission reference, your own profile and password, and the audit log. **Administrators only** can create, edit, deactivate or reset passwords for staff accounts.

## Part C: Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Forgot member portal password | Contact the Trust office — resets are handled manually for security |
| "We could not verify your details" when signing up | The Trust already holds a record for that email but your ID number and phone do not match it. Call 081 391 7967 or email themhezatrust@gmail.com |
| "An account already exists for this email address" | Sign in at **Login → Member** instead of signing up |
| Portal shows no plot or no payments yet | Normal for a new account — a consultant confirms your allocation and records payments after sign-up |
| Payment not showing in portal | Check your EFT reference format; email proof of payment to themhezatrust@gmail.com |
| "You do not have permission" in workstation | Your role can view that module but not change it — ask Bongani Sifiniza (Administrator) |
| Session expired | Normal after 12 hours; sign in again |
| Document upload fails | Max file size is 10 MB; use PDF/JPG/PNG where possible |
