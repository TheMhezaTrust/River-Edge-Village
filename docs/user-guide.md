# User Guide — The Mheza Trust Website & Workstation

## Part A: Public Website Users

### Finding out what is available
1. **Individual plots are not published on the public website.** There is no plot list, no interactive farm map, no availability colours and no per-erf pricing outside the Workstation: erf numbers, sizes and prices belong to each member's signed contract, so they are staff-only and `/plots` returns *404 Not found* for anyone not signed in. The public site also does not state how many plots are left.
2. What the public site **does** tell you, from the navigation bar:
   - **Projects → River Edge Rural Village** — the development itself: 31.9 hectares of erven of about 800 m² for 165 families, the subdivision and infrastructure progress, the approach to pricing, and the **Express Interest or Buy a Plot** form at the foot of the page.
   - **Gallery** — photographs of the site.
   - **Status** — the project timeline and public announcements.
   - **Trust Info** — the Trust's registration, governance and banking details.
   - **Terms** — the full Terms and Conditions of Sale, including the Right of Use, the payment ladder and the mandatory payment reference.
   - **About Us** (with **How Our Projects Work**) and **Contact**.
3. To find out which erven are still available and what one would cost, **contact the office** — phone **081 391 7967**, email **themhezatrust@gmail.com**, or one of the forms below. Availability and price are quoted per erf, because size, position and the date of purchase all differ.

### Expressing interest / buying
- **On the project page** (`/projects/river-edge` → *Express Interest or Buy a Plot*): full name and surname, ID or passport number, email, phone and a message. No plot is attached to this form — say in the message what you are looking for and the office will come back to you with what is available.
- **On Contact Us**, three separate forms: **Send Us a Message** (adds a subject line), **Register Your Interest** (news about promotions, price changes and new projects, plus a general plot preference such as a corner plot or any available plot) and **Schedule a Site Viewing** (preferred date and number of attendees — viewings are by appointment during office hours, Mon–Fri).
- After submitting you see a confirmation on screen. The inquiry is logged in the Trust's Workstation and an email notification goes to themhezatrust@gmail.com; a consultant contacts you within 24–48 hours. Staff can reply to you from the inquiry itself, and every reply is kept in that inquiry's communication history.
- The ID or passport number you enter is kept on the inquiry record only — it is never copied into the mailbox.
- The purchase itself is completed with the office: they confirm an available erf with you, capture your details against the signed contract (price, payment plan and purchase date), and then issue your portal access so you can see your own plot, payments and statement.

### Checking project status
The public **Status** page shows the project timeline and public announcements filterable by category. Detailed government department engagement records (contact officials, direct numbers, internal notes and follow-up dates) are confidential and only visible to staff inside the Workstation.

### Member portal (your account is created for you, then you log in)
1. **Your account is created by the Trust office.** There is no public sign-up form. To get portal access, contact the Trust by phone (**081 391 7967**) or email (**themhezatrust@gmail.com**); staff verify your identity and set up your member record.
2. **Getting a password.** The office does one of two things:
   - **Emails you an access link** — a single-use link, valid for **30 minutes**, that opens a page where you choose your own password. If the link has expired, ask the office to send another, or use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
   - **Gives you a temporary password** (by phone or in person) — the portal will make you replace it before anything else opens.
3. **Log in** at **Member Login** (top right of every public page, `/portal`) using your registered email address and password.
4. **Forgot your password?** Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. The Trust emails a reset link to the address on your member record; it works once and expires after 30 minutes. For security the page always says a link is on its way, whether or not the address is on the register — if nothing arrives in a few minutes, the address is probably not the one the office holds, so call them.
5. **You are signed out automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.** A minute before it happens a notice appears at the bottom of the screen with a **Stay signed in** button. Simply sign in again afterwards — nothing is lost.
6. **You must be logged in to see any of your information.** Nothing about your payments or balance is visible to the public or to any other member. In the portal you can:
   - **Dashboard** — what you have paid, what is outstanding, your plot, latest Trust announcements, and the EFT details with your mandatory payment reference
   - **My Profile** — see everything the Trust holds about you (name, ID number, date of birth, gender, marital status, both addresses, contact number, email). You can change your **contact number, physical address, postal address and email** yourself; changing your email asks for your current password, because your email is how you sign in. **Your name and ID number cannot be edited** — use the *Request a name or ID correction* form and a member of the Trust office approves it (you may be asked for a certified ID copy). Your password is changed here too. This page also shows your **Date joined** — the purchase date the Trust holds for you; if it does not match your signed contract, ask the office to correct it.
   - **My Beneficiaries** — the nominations the Trust currently holds. A nomination has to be signed, so it cannot be edited in the portal: get a **Beneficiary Nomination Form** from the office, sign it and hand it in. Downloading the blank form and uploading a signed copy from the portal is being added next.
   - **My Plot Details** — project name, plot number, block, size, purchase price, the **date of purchase** for that erf, what you have paid on that plot, and your **Right of Use Status**, which reads either **Granted (Certificate Pending)** or **Active (Certificate Issued)**. The status depends on whether the Trust has issued your Certificate of Land Use, not on whether the plot is paid off — the payment position is shown separately as *Paid on this plot* and *Balance on this plot*. Every plot starts at **Granted (Certificate Pending)**; the Trust issues certificates itself and will notify you, and the scanned certificate is filed under **My Documents** once it has. If you hold more than one erf, each one is listed separately with its own price and purchase date.
   - **Payments & Statement** — every payment the office has recorded (date, amount, reference, method, plot, status), your **total paid** and **outstanding balance**, and a **Download PDF Statement** button.
   - **My Documents** — the signed paperwork the Trust holds for you, above all your stamped **Acknowledgement of Payment and Plot Allocation**, and your Certificate of Land Use once it has been issued. Each entry shows its title, category, the date it was added and its file size, with **View** (opens the PDF in a new tab) and **Download** (saves a copy). Keep your own copy: this is what proves your plot allocation and the payments you have made. Only you and Trust staff can see these files — they are served through the portal, not from a public link, so forwarding the web address does not give anybody else access. If you have signed a document and it is not listed here, call the office on **081 391 7967**.
7. **Your statement.** *Download PDF Statement* opens a formal statement of account on Trust letterhead — statement number, your details, your date of purchase, your plot(s), a running schedule of payments with the balance after each one, totals, and the banking details if you still owe something. Use your browser's **Print → Save as PDF** in the dialog that opens; the page is already laid out for A4 and the sidebar and buttons drop out of the printed copy. Keep it, or hand it to your bank or attorney.
8. Payments are made by EFT to the project's official Absa account — **River Edge SA Primary Co-operative Ltd**, acc **4121013447**, branch **632005** (shown in the portal and on **Trust Info**). Always use the mandatory reference format `Plot Number – Buyer Name` (for example `12 – Your Full Name`), as set out in clause 6.4 of the Terms and Conditions of Sale — payments without it cannot be allocated.
9. **Something wrong on your record?** If a payment you made is not showing, email your proof of payment to **themhezatrust@gmail.com** and quote your plot number. Uploading proof of payment straight into the portal is being added next; until then the office captures it for you.

### Terms and Conditions of Sale
The full 21-clause **Terms and Conditions of Sale** are published at `/terms` (also linked from the footer, the nav bar, Trust Info and every plot page). They cover the Right of Use, pricing and the non-payment ladder, the Right of Use Certificate and transfer fee, beneficiary nomination, the Community Constitution, building setbacks and deadlines, infrastructure requirements, annual levies, privacy, dispute resolution and termination. Read them before paying a deposit.

### Verifying the Trust
The **Trust Info** page lists the Trust registration number (IT000099/2024(E)), trustees, a deed summary, and official banking details. All The Mheza Trust projects are administered by **River Edge Primary Co-Op**. The Trust will never ask you to pay into a personal account. Verify by phone before paying.

## Part B: Workstation Users (Staff)

### Signing in
- URL: `/login` and choose **Administrator / Staff** (or go directly to `/workstation`) — authorized personnel only. All actions are recorded in the audit log.
- Use your work email and password. After 12 hours your session expires.
- **Forgot password?** below the password field opens the self-service recovery flow. It only works for the staff account that has recovery questions on file; everyone else must ask an Administrator to reset their password in Settings → Users.
- **Every role can open and read every module.** What differs is what you can *change*: action buttons are hidden automatically when your role cannot edit that area.

### Who can change what

| Role | People | Can change |
|---|---|---|
| Administrator | Bongani Sifiniza | Everything, including staff accounts, settings and the audit log |
| Finance & Member Records | Thandile Sifiniza, Sisanda Toni, Nokuthula Gedle | Finances, payroll, member/customer records, inquiries, members' document vaults, member communications and announcements |
| Plot Administrator | Sipho Jauka, Sydney Velapi, Lihle Jacob | Plot records: erf number, size, availability, price, block, date of purchase and layout position |

All three roles can view every module (members, plots, finance, documents, departments, inquiries, tasks, communication, reports, settings) and can update their own tasks. Where you cannot edit, the page shows a **"Read-only view"** banner and the add/edit/delete buttons are not rendered.

One action is narrower than the table suggests: **editing or deleting a payment that has already been recorded** is Administrator-only. Finance & Member Records can still capture new payments, but cannot rewrite or remove history, because that changes the Trust's recorded income.

A member's document vault follows the table exactly: every role can open and download a member's signed documents, while only Administrators and Finance & Member Records can upload or delete them — and both of those ask for your password first.

### Dashboard
Shows quick stats (plots by status, members, outstanding balances, monthly revenue, sales progress), a 12-month sales trend chart, quick actions, your notifications, your assigned tasks, and recent team activity.

### Members
- **Members** — search/filter all members; export CSV; add a member with full personal, plot and payment-plan details; open a member for their complete record.
- On a member's page: record payments (auto-creates an income entry and notifies the team), edit details, file their signed documents (below), beneficiaries and notes.
- **Member Contracts** — the signed and stamped paperwork the office issues to this member, above all the **Acknowledgement of Payment and Plot Allocation**.
  - **Upload PDF** asks for a title, a category and the scan. Give it a title the member will recognise, and add the plot number where a member holds more than one erf (for example *Acknowledgement of Payment and Plot Allocation — Plot 45*).
  - You re-enter your password, then the file travels from your browser straight into the Trust's private Vercel Blob storage — PDF only, up to 25 MB, with a progress bar. It appears in the member's portal under **My Documents** the moment the upload finishes.
  - Each row shows the category, the date added, the file size and who uploaded it, with **Download** and **Delete**. Click the title to open the PDF in a new tab.
  - **Delete** asks for your password and removes the file straight away — the member loses access the moment you confirm, so only delete a scan you are about to replace.
  - Uploading and deleting need the document-management right (**Administrators** and **Finance & Member Records**). Other roles can open and download a member's documents but cannot change the vault.
  - Nothing here is a public link. Every download is served through a route that first checks who is asking, and a member can only ever reach their own files — another member's document does not exist as far as their portal is concerned.
- **Correcting a record against the signed contract.** Prices, sizes and payment histories are *not* uniform — members bought at different times and some hold more than one erf — so everything on this page can be overridden:
  - **Edit** (member details) includes **Date Joined / Date of Purchase** and a **Purchase Price Override**. Leave the override empty and the member is charged the sum of their plot prices; set a figure and *that* figure is what every balance is calculated from, on this page and in the member's portal. When an override is in force the Plot Information card says so in amber, so you are never left wondering why a plot price change did not move the balance.
  - **Edit plot** on each plot in the Plot Information card changes that erf's number, block, size (m²), price and its own **Date of Purchase** — a member who bought two erven a year apart has two dates and usually two prices. Changing a **plot number** warns you first: it moves the erf off its position on the survey-plan layout and breaks the `Plot Number – Buyer Name` bank reference convention (T&C 6.4) for payments already recorded against it.
  - **Assign a plot** attaches an available erf to this member (it becomes Sold and its price joins their balance immediately). **Unassign** releases it back to Available and drops it from their balance — it asks for confirmation first, because it changes what the member owes.
  - Every change is written to the audit log and appears in the member's portal the next time they open it.
- **Correcting a past payment** — each row in **Payment History** has **Edit** and **Delete** for Administrators. You can change the date, amount, method, bank reference, note and which of the member's plots the payment is allocated to. Because this rewrites money the Trust has already banked, it is restricted to **Administrator** (Finance & Member Records can still *add* payments but cannot rewrite or remove history), your password is re-confirmed every time, and the matching income record moves with the payment so the cashflow report and the member's statement cannot disagree. Deleting a payment removes its income entry too. Payments captured before this link existed cannot be matched automatically — if that happens the page tells you to correct the income row by hand under Finance → Income.
- **Member Portal Access** (on the member's page, needs member-management rights and your password re-entered):
  - **Email an access link** — sends the member a single-use link, valid 30 minutes, to choose their own password. Use this whenever the member has a working email address; it means nobody has to read a password out over the phone. On a member who already has a password the button reads **Email a reset link**. If outbound email is not configured the panel says so — use the next option instead.
  - **Set temporary password** — generates a 12-character password shown **once**, with a copy button. Hand it over privately. The member cannot see anything in the portal until they replace it, so it is safe if it is written down briefly.
  - The panel also shows whether the member can sign in yet and when they last did.
- **Portal change requests** — a member cannot change their own name or ID number, so those arrive as requests. A panel at the top of **Members** lists everything waiting. **Approve** writes the corrected value straight onto the member record; **Reject** leaves it as it is. Both ask for your password first, and every decision is written to the audit log. The member sees the new status the next time they open **My Profile**.

### Plots
- Map and table views of all 216 plots, numbered per the official survey layout plan (including subdivided erven such as 1A, 145A/145B, 165A, 186A and 203A). Click a plot to change status (Available / Reserved / Sold), edit size, price or details, and set its **Date of Purchase** — the date this erf was bought, which is what the member sees on their own plot page and statement. Members who bought more than one erf often bought them on different dates and at different prices, so the date belongs to the plot, not only to the member.
- Select multiple rows to bulk-update status or price.
- **Certificate of Land Use.** Clicking a plot also shows its **Right of Use** — *Granted (Certificate Pending)* or *Active (Certificate Issued)* — which is what the member sees in their portal. Use **Mark certificate issued** when the Trust issues the certificate for that erf; the date and time are recorded and the member's portal switches to *Active (Certificate Issued)*. **Undo certificate issued** reverses it if you clicked in error. This is separate from the plot's Sold/Reserved status and from its payment balance: a plot can be fully paid and still be *Certificate Pending*. Only **Administrator** and **Plot Administrator** can change it — the same roles that can edit plots at all.

### Inquiries
- All website and phone inquiries land here. Assign to a team member, change status (New → Contacted → Converted/Closed), set follow-up dates, and **convert to member** (pre-fills the member record and reserves the plot).
- When a visitor submits a website form, the inquiry appears here and an email is sent to themhezatrust@gmail.com with the name, contact details, plot of interest and message, plus a link straight to this page. The ID/passport a visitor enters is kept on the inquiry record only — it is never copied into the mailbox.
- **Reply to Customer** — open an inquiry with **View**, then **Reply to Customer**, type the message and press **Send**. The email goes out from the Trust's mailing address and the customer's answer arrives in themhezatrust@gmail.com.
- Every send is listed under **Communication History** on that inquiry: who sent it, when, the subject, the full message, and whether it was **Sent** or **Failed** (a failed attempt shows the reason, so you know the customer was never reached).
- A successful send moves a **New** inquiry to **Contacted** automatically. An inquiry already **Converted** or **Closed** keeps its status, so the conversion rate on this page stays correct.
- Only Administrator and Finance & Member Records can send. If an inquiry was logged by phone with no email address, the button tells you a reply cannot be sent — use the phone number instead.

### Finance
- **Income / Expenses**: record and review all transactions with categories and totals. An income row that was generated from a member payment cannot be deleted here — correct or delete the payment on the member's page instead, so the member's statement and the books move together.
- **Payments**: outstanding balances per member with full payment history.
- **Cash Flow**: 12-month inflow/outflow chart, closing balances, and P&L summary.
- **Payroll**: employees with PAYE/UIF/SDL deductions, net pay, and printable payslips.

### Documents
The central Trust-level library (folders for Trust, Project, Legal, Financial, Member, Department and Minutes) is **switched off for now**, and its screen is not in the sidebar. A member's own signed paperwork is filed instead under **Member Contracts** on their record — see *Members* above — which is what the member sees in their portal under **My Documents**.

### Departments
Every government engagement (BCMM, DALRRD, Sanitation, Waterworks, IEM, City Health, DWA, DEDEA, Roads, Engineering) with contact person, status, follow-up dates and a communication log. Log every email, call or meeting here — it feeds the public Status page narrative.

### Tasks
List, drag-and-drop **Kanban**, and calendar views. Assign tasks, set priorities and due dates; overdue items are highlighted; assignees get notified.

### Communication
Internal messages between staff (inbox/sent/drafts), team announcements (Administrator and Finance & Member Records roles), and copy-ready templates for payment reminders and follow-ups.

### Reports
Sales by month, inquiry conversion funnel, member payment status, expense breakdown, and CSV export.

### Traffic Analytics
Visible to one designated administrator account only (the link does not appear for anyone else, and the page returns "not found" for every other role). Shows public-website traffic: **unique visitors** and **page views** for today, this week, this month, this year and all time; a **30-day trend chart**; and a **most visited pages** breakdown with a switch between this month and this year. Only public pages are counted — the workstation, the member portal, the sign-in pages and the administrator plot browser are never recorded. Visitors are counted from a hashed IP address, so no personal data is stored.

### Settings
Open to every role: the staff directory (read-only unless you are an Administrator), the role permission reference, your own profile and password, and the audit log. **Administrators only** can create, edit, deactivate or reset passwords for staff accounts. The Profile tab also holds **Password Recovery Questions** for the designated administrator account: two questions and answers used by the Forgot password? flow. Answers are stored encrypted and are never shown again — write them down, and note that changing them requires your current password plus all four values.

## Part C: Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Forgot member portal password | Use **Forgot password?** on `/portal` — a reset link goes to the address the Trust holds. Nothing arrived? The address on the record is probably different, so call the office on 081 391 7967 and they will email a new link or give you a temporary password |
| Member portal access link "is no longer valid" | The link works once and expires after 30 minutes. Ask for a new one from **Forgot password?**, or have the office issue a fresh link from the member's page |
| Member signs in and is taken straight to a "choose a new password" page | Normal after a temporary password — the portal will not open until it is replaced |
| Forgot workstation password | Use **Forgot password?** on `/workstation/login` if your account has recovery questions on file; otherwise ask an Administrator to reset it in Settings → Users |
| Recovery says recovery is locked for another X minutes | Five incorrect answers were given — wait out the 30-minute lock, then start again |
| Reply to Customer says outbound email is not configured | The Resend API key is missing from the hosting environment. Ask whoever manages Vercel to set `RESEND_API_KEY`, then try again — nothing was sent and nothing was logged as sent |
| Email an access link says outbound email is not configured | Same cause. Use **Set temporary password** on the member's page instead and hand it over privately |
| A reply is listed as **Failed** in Communication History | The email did not reach the customer; the reason is shown under the message. Check the address, then send again |
| A website inquiry is in the list but no notification email arrived | The inquiry itself is safe — it is saved before the email is attempted. Ask whoever manages Vercel to check the function logs for `office notification for #… failed`; the usual cause is a missing `RESEND_API_KEY` or an unverified sending domain |
| "Invalid email or password" at the portal | Either the password is wrong, or the office has not issued one for that address yet. Use **Forgot password?**, or call 081 391 7967 — member accounts are created by the Trust office, there is no public sign-up |
| "Too many sign-in attempts" at the portal | Eight wrong attempts in five minutes. Wait five minutes, then use **Forgot password?** rather than guessing again |
| "This member account is inactive" | The office has deactivated the record. Call them |
| Portal shows no plot or no payments yet | Normal for a new account — a consultant confirms your allocation and records payments |
| Payment not showing in portal | Check your EFT reference format; email proof of payment to themhezatrust@gmail.com |
| Name or ID number is wrong in the portal and cannot be edited | Use **Request a name or ID correction** on **My Profile** — the Trust office approves it. Keep an eye on the status shown under the form |
| "You do not have permission" in workstation | Your role can view that module but not change it — ask Bongani Sifiniza (Administrator) |
| Signed out of the member portal | Normal after **15 minutes** of inactivity; sign in again. Staff sessions run 12 hours |
| Document upload fails | Max file size is 10 MB; use PDF/JPG/PNG where possible |
